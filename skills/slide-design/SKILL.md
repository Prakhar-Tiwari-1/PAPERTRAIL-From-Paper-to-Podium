## Structure (assertion-evidence)
- The **title is a full-sentence claim**; the **body is the evidence**: a figure, a table excerpt, a diagram, a worked example or one big number. Not a wall of bullets.
- **Every slide except the title, quiz and workflow slides carries a visual from the paper.** At most TWO main slides may be bullets only.
- One idea per slide. **Visible text: aim for 50 to 70 words, never above 70** (title, bullets, captions and table cells count). At most 4 bullets of at most 12 words.
- Real figures with source captions ("Smith et al., 2024, Fig. N" using the paper's citation, or "Our analysis, artifacts/X.csv"). A table excerpt shows 3 to 6 rows and at most 6 columns: the rows that carry the point.
- **Balanced space.** The visual is large and the slide is used: a slide whose lower third is empty, or whose figure floats in a corner, is a defect. Fix it with a visual
  (diagram, chart, example, or a table plus a one-line reading of it), never with more text. Split a slide rather than shrink text. Body text never below 16 px.
- The theme provides colour (navy titles, orange rule, tinted panels). Do not add colours, styles or decoration.

## Plain writing
Write the way a careful student speaks: short, concrete, specific, with the paper's own terms and numbers. A check scans slides AND speaker notes and sends the deck back for
stock AI vocabulary (delve, unlock, seamless, cutting-edge, landscape of, plays a crucial role, it is worth noting), "not just X but Y", "it is not X, it is Y", "in conclusion",
exclamation marks and em dashes. No summary sentence that repeats the slide, no hedging adverbs, no triplets of adjectives.

## Banned
Emoji; decorative icons; gradients, glows, shadows; stock images; three-card feature grids; titles like "Introduction", "Overview", "Key Takeaways", "Conclusion";
"game-changer", "revolutionize", "in today's fast-paced", "robust" as praise; rhetorical-question titles; bold scattered through sentences; dashes as decoration.

## Rule -> SlideAudit category -> Gestalt principle
| Rule | SlideAudit category it prevents | Gestalt principle |
|---|---|---|
| at most 70 words, at most 4 bullets | excessive text density | Proximity |
| flat theme colours only | overuse of or inconsistent colour | Similarity |
| 64 px margins | unbalanced space or margin usage | Symmetry |
| grid alignment | misaligned elements | Continuity |
| claim title plus one evidence block | lack of visual hierarchy | Figure-Ground |
| no decorative icons | irrelevant or off-topic visuals | Common Fate |
| body at least 16 px, titles 40 px | inappropriate font sizing | Figure-Ground |
| contrast at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for text at least 24 px) | insufficient contrast | Figure-Ground |
| same layouts for the same job | inconsistent layout across slides | Similarity |

Use these category names when discussing problems, so the author and the critics speak the same language.
