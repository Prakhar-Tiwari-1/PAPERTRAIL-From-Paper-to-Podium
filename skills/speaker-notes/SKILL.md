---
name: speaker-notes
description: Style and length of speaker notes.
stages: [author, reviser, oneshot]
---
- Spoken, not written: contractions, short sentences, no bullet fragments. Plain words; the notes are scanned for AI-sounding phrasing like the slides (no
  "delve", "crucial role", "not just X but Y", no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no "in conclusion").
- Length: planned minutes x 140 words per slide. The main deck totals 2,100 to 2,900 words, which is 15 to 20 minutes. If the total is short, add the
  missing step of an explanation, a concrete example from the paper, or what to look at in the figure; never filler.
- Say what the slide shows, why it matters, then the number or the point, then what to notice. Point at the visual ("the orange bars", "the first
  user turn") so the audience follows.
- Numbers are said the way they appear on the slide. Notes may explain, but must not introduce a number or claim that is not on the slide or in
  deck_claims.
- End every slide's notes with one sentence that leads into the next slide.
- The quiz slide: about 400 words. Ask the questions one by one, tell the speaker to pause for the audience, then reveal the answers; say a little more on the
  three most instructive ones, including one what-if.
- The Tools & Workflow slide: about 150 words on the harness, the stages, which models did what, and what the critics caught.
- Put the notes in an HTML comment at the end of the slide.
